Guides

Shop smarter, not just greener

🔍

Spot greenwashing

Vague claims like "eco-friendly" mean little. Look for certifications, specific numbers and third-party audits.

📦

Packaging matters

A great product in plastic wrap loses points. Prioritise refillable, compostable or plastic-free packaging.

⏳

Buy for longevity

The greenest product is the one you keep for years. Durability and repairability beat a low price every time.
