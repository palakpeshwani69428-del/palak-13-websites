Why it works

1

Slow, deep breathing activates your parasympathetic nervous system — the body's built-in calm-down response.

2

Extending your exhale longer than your inhale signals safety to your brain and slows your heart rate.

3

Just two minutes is enough to lower stress hormones. Try it before sleep or a difficult conversation.
